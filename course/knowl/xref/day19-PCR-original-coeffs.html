Task 108.b.

Assume the trained model is given by \(y=2 Z_1 - Z_2\text{.}\) Write down this model in terms of the \(p\) original predictors.
🔗
Hint.
The linear transformation from the \(X\) s to \(Z_m\) is given by
\begin{equation*} Z_m = \sum_{j=1}^{p}\phi_{jm}X_j. \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} y \amp= 2(X_1+3X_2+0X_3)-(-X_1+\tfrac{1}{2}X_2+\tfrac{1}{2}X_3) \\ \amp= 3X_1+2.5X_2-0.5X_3 \end{align*}
