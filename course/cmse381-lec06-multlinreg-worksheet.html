    11.
    (a)
    What are the null and alternative hypotheses for the test this statistic is used for?
    🔗
    Hint 1.
    The global F-test asks whether the predictor slopes are jointly zero.
    🔗
    🔗
    Hint 2.
    The alternative only needs one slope to be nonzero; it does not require every predictor to be useful.
    🔗
    🔗
    Hint 3.
    Common error: The null concerns the three slope coefficients, not the intercept, and the alternative is not that all three slopes are nonzero.
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(H_0:\beta_{TV}=\beta_{Radio}=\beta_{Newspaper}=0\text{.}\) The alternative is \(H_A:\) at least one of these three slope coefficients is nonzero.

    import itertools

variables = ['TV', 'Radio', 'Newspaper']
results = []
for size in range(1, len(variables) + 1):
    for subset in itertools.combinations(variables, size):
        formula = 'Sales ~ ' + ' + '.join(subset)
        fitted = smf.ols(formula, data=advertising_df).fit()
        results.append((subset, fitted.ssr))

for subset, rss in results:
    print(subset, round(rss, 2))

print('Smallest:', min(results, key=lambda item: item[1]))
