Worksheet Exercise 3. A Cubic Spline with One Knot.

Consider a cubic spline with a knot at \(c_1=4\text{.}\) Its basis functions are \(b_1(x)=x\text{,}\) \(b_2(x)=x^2\text{,}\) \(b_3(x)=x^3\text{,}\) and \(b_4(x)=h(x,4)\text{,}\) where \(h(x,4)=(x-4)^3\) for \(x>4\) and \(h(x,4)=0\) otherwise. The learned model is \(f(x)=3+b_1(x)-2b_2(x)+3b_3(x)-4b_4(x)\text{.}\)
🔗

(a)

Write the equation of \(f(x)\) as a piecewise polynomial on either side of \(x=4\text{.}\) Draw its graph.

🔗

(b)

What conditions must a piecewise polynomial satisfy to be a cubic spline?
