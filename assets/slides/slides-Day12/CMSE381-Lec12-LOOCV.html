Section 2

Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV)
The idea

⋮
The idea in mathy words

  • Remove (x1,y1) for testing.
  • Train the model on n − 1 points: {(x2,y2),⋯,(xn,yn)}
  • Calculate MSE1 = (y1 −ŷ1)2
  • Remove (x2,y2) for testing.
  • Train the model on n − 1 points: {(x1,y1),(x3,y3),⋯,(xn,yn)}
  • Calculate MSE2 = (y2 −ŷ2)2
  • Rinse and repeat

Return the score:

CV(n) = 1 n∑ i=1nMSE i
